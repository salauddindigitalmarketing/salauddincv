Best Digital Marketer in Dhaka: Real Campaigns, Real Numbers

By Salauddin Ahmed 24 November 2026 5 min read

"Best" is a big word, and in digital marketing it gets thrown around a lot. I won't ask you to take it on trust. Here is what the word should mean — measurable results, at a cost you can check — and here are three real campaigns from my own work in Dhaka, with the numbers exactly as Ads Manager reported them.

What "best" should mean

Not followers. Not promises. The only honest measure of a marketer is cost per result — and whether the result is a click, a conversation, or a customer. If a marketer cannot show you the numbers behind the claim, the word means nothing. That is the standard I hold myself to, and it is the standard I suggest you hold anyone to — including me. (I wrote a longer version of that advice in how to choose a digital marketer in Dhaka.)

Campaign 1 — Ahmed Food Products: clicks at $0.004

For Ahmed Food Products, where I work as Digital Marketing Specialist, a link-click campaign delivered 3,951 clicks at $0.004 per click, reaching 49,599 people. What made it work was not a trick: a clear offer, creative written in Bangla, and tight local targeting. Food is visual, so the ad showed the real product — not a stock photo. The audience was people in and around Dhaka who actually buy these products.

Campaign 2 — 288 Messenger conversations at $0.10

The second campaign for Ahmed Food took a different route: 288 Messenger conversations at $0.10 each, some as low as $0.055. In Bangladesh, customers often prefer messaging a page to filling in a form, so the campaign met them where they already were. A conversation is worth more than a click — it is a person asking about your product — and at ten cents each, it is a conversation a small business can afford to have at scale.

Campaign 3 — 20,080 video views at $0.002

For South Wind Engineering, my previous employer, a video Leads campaign delivered 20,080 ThruPlays at $0.002 each, reaching 68,894 people on just $34.86 of spend. Video worked because the product — engineering services — needed explaining, and a short video did that better than a static image. An awareness campaign alongside it reached 18,856 people for $1.19, with 630 people remembering the ad afterwards.

The pattern behind the numbers

Three campaigns, three different businesses, but the same three habits in all of them:

  • Start small, then scale. Every campaign began with a small budget. Only the ones that earned their keep got more.
  • Measure cost per result, not likes. What counts is where the click ends up: a conversation, a lead, or a customer.
  • Creative that fits the audience. Bangla copy, local targeting, real products — nothing bought, nothing faked.

Judge for yourself

The numbers above are on my projects page, and the method behind them is in the posts on this blog — including when to use Google Ads and when to use Meta Ads. If you are looking for a digital marketer in Dhaka, don't take anyone's word for "best" — mine included. Ask for the numbers, check the method, and then decide.
