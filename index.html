In Detail

What each service actually includes

A closer look at the six services — and what each one delivers for a business in Dhaka.

SEO

Search engine optimisation that puts a Dhaka business in front of people already looking for it. I start with a technical audit — site speed, mobile experience, and indexation — then build keyword research around how Bangladeshi customers actually search, and fix the on-page issues holding rankings back. Every change is tracked in Google Search Console and GA4, so progress is measured in traffic and enquiries, not guesses.

Digital Marketing

Paid campaigns planned around cost per result, not clicks alone. My Meta Ads work for a Bangladeshi food brand brought 3,951 link clicks at $0.004 each and 288 Messenger conversations at $0.10 each, while a video leads campaign reached 68,894 people on $34.86 of spend. Google Ads captures the people searching right now; Meta Ads finds the people most likely to buy next.

Web Development

Fast, responsive websites that make marketing work harder. A slow or confusing site wastes every taka spent on ads, so I build clean, mobile-first pages that load quickly and guide visitors toward one clear action — a call, a message, or a purchase. Every site ships with tracking in place, so you can see exactly which channel brought the enquiry.

Social Media Management

Facebook and Instagram content and campaigns that keep a brand in front of its audience every day. I plan the calendar, write the posts, and run the ads behind them — then report what each post actually did. One recent campaign added 1,478 new followers at $0.013 each, and video posts earned 11,646 ThruPlays at under $0.001 each.

Branding & Design

Logos and visuals that make a business recognisable at a glance — on a shop sign, a product pack, or a phone screen. Good design is not decoration; it is the first thing a customer trusts. I design for consistency, so the Facebook post, the website, and the packaging all look like the same brand.

Content Strategy

Content planned around what your customers ask, not what you feel like posting. I research the questions people search for, then turn them into articles, posts, and videos that answer them — the same approach behind this site's blog and my LinkedIn and Medium articles. Good content keeps working long after the ad budget stops.
